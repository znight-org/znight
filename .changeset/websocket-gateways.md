New `@znight/websocket` package (RFC 0003): WebSocket gateways served by the HTTP server, `@Gateway` classes with `@OnMessage` handlers, handshake and message guards, Standard Schema payload validation, rooms and broadcasts, backpressure, rate limits and heartbeats, checked at build time; native transport on Bun, `ws` on Node.js (`@znight/websocket/ws`), and `@znight/websocket/testing`.
